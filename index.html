12 october · for you

Happy birthday,
Aru.

I wanted to do a little more than just text you happy birthday. I spent longer on this than I expected, but I wanted to make something you’d actually like.

You already know I like talking to you. We spend enough time arguing and trying to get a reaction out of each other that I probably don’t say that very clearly. But I do. Even when we have absolutely nothing to talk about, I still enjoy talking to you.

I like hearing whatever you have to tell me, whether it’s something about your day or a completely random thought. Though I’m sure you’ll find something in this note to argue with too.

I remembered the little flipbook you made me and wanted you to have something to keep as well. So here’s your own little birthday note, in blue.

I wish I could say happy birthday in person. Sorry I couldn’t do more for your birthday, but I hope this little note makes you smile.

I hope today goes exactly how you want it to. And I hope seventeen brings you things you’re excited about, good surprises, and plenty of days you’ll want to tell me about.

Have a really good birthday, Aru. I’m happy you’re in my life.

Gaurav
